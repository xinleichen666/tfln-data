The transmission spectrum of the bare LN ring resonator measured using a grating spectrometer (Fig. 1d) yields an apparent quality factor of 3.8 × 104. To obtain a more accurate value, we performed high-resolution measurements using a mode-hop-free narrow-linewidth laser, revealing a loaded quality factor of 2.52 × 105 at \~912 nm. This corresponds to an intrinsic LN propagation loss of *α*LN = 1.37 dB cm-1(see Supplementary Fig. S2). Since the coupling loss *α*M is related to the mode transformer efficiency: *α*M = - 10 × log10*η*/*L*M, with *L*M being the length of the mode transformer. In our design, a 10.5 μm-long taper achieves a coupling loss of *η* = 98.2%, corresponding to *α*M = 28.5 dB cm−1.
