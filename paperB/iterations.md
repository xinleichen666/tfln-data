Iter-5 caveats: (1) the AO drive strength comes from a linear extrapolation of published resonant VpiL values (Shao 2019 MZI 4.6 V over 100 um, single arm; Ni 2026 1.004 V cm push-pull) to a ring that is 33% (A) or 15% (B) covered by the acoustic field. (2) The shift frequency is fixed by the acoustic resonance (0.84 or 3.33 GHz), with RF bandwidth set by the acoustic Q (~1 MHz in B), while Hu reaches 11-28 GHz with >3 GHz bandwidth. (3) IL is 1.8 dB vs 0.45 dB measured by Hu, and absolute efficiency is 65%. (4) We did not model acoustic heating or optical loss from the IDT or metal. (5) Thermal phonons only add incoherent phase noise of about n_th/N_coh, which is negligible.
Data: data/iter5_*.json/csv, code iter5_aomolecule.py, iter5b_scan.py.
