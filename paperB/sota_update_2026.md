## Not found
- No 2022–2026 measured EO coupled-ring TFLN frequency shifter with lower RF power than Hu 2021 (96 mW, 98.7%) was found. Hu remains the measured EO baseline (search was limited; Google Scholar not queried directly).
- No demonstrated on-chip microwave-resonator-enhanced EO frequency shifter (loaded Q~450) was found; our "in principle could match, not demonstrated" statement still stands.
- No measured acousto-optic frequency shifter at µW RF with >90% shift efficiency found.

## Verdict
- 13,000x vs Hu measured: not undercut by any newer measured EO shifter found.
- 440x matched-EO: not undercut; N2's measured LNOS coupling (α=1774 GHz²/W) supports µW-scale acoustic coupling being realistic, Checked iter5_aomolecule.py: our 7.4 µW is RF power, P=V²/(2·50 Ω), with V from measured IDT-referred V_πL (Shao 2019), so IDT transduction is implicitly included; but it assumes the full source voltage appears across the IDT (no impedance-mismatch loss). State this explicitly; IDT mismatch is an additional caveat beyond the EO-side one.
- Novelty: N1 (USTC, Zou group) already demonstrated an acoustically driven TFLN photonic molecule with frequency conversion. Must be cited and the claim reframed as "high-efficiency, single-output frequency shifter at µW drive" rather than "first acoustic photonic molecule".
