**Manuscript working title (EN):** Frequency-bin purity and HOM visibility bounds for acousto-optic frequency shifters: a room-temperature noise budget

## Abstract

We cast residual carrier and sidebands of on-chip acousto-optic frequency shifters as frequency-bin purity and derive the room-temperature Hong–Ou–Mandel (HOM) visibility upper bound versus carrier suppression. Insertion loss and on-chip diffraction efficiency enter only a coincidence-rate factor, not the visibility bound. For traveling-wave Bragg devices (Type A), Shao *et al.*, *Opt. Express* **28**, 23728–23738 (2020) supplies the device model: the main figure shows theoretical \(V_\mathrm{upper}(R_c)\), marks planned commercial fiber-AOM self-tests by \(R_c\) abscissae only, and places a vertical literature anchor at Shao’s \(\approx 31\,\mathrm{dB}\) carrier suppression as **deflection-port frequency-bin purity**, explicitly **not** a benchtop HOM visibility. Resonant transducers (Type B) are locked to Shao *et al.*, *Optica* **6**, 1498–1505 (2019) Table S2 (\(\Omega/2\pi=2.007\,\mathrm{GHz}\)) for \(n_\mathrm{th}\) and an added-noise bound only—no frequency-bin HOM main curve. Brillouin / dynamic-Bragg devices (Type C) use a model-assumption spontaneous factor; Zhu *et al.* (arXiv:2511.22585) \(R=24\%\) implies \(V_\mathrm{upper}\approx0.019\) at room temperature. The SPDC band and AOM model are locked to \(\sim1550\,\mathrm{nm}\) and a G\&H Fiber-Q 80 MHz unit; measured cells remain empty pending classical calibration and SPDC HOM. **Internal use only (research group): simulation and scheme working draft; measurements pending; no fabricated data; Fiber-Q not ordered; no in-house fab; not for public citation. No third-party verify/ PDFs in repo.** We do not claim near-unity benchtop visibility, liquid-helium primary results, or parity with electro-optic \(\sim99\%\) conversion (Hu *et al.*, *Nature* **599**, 587–593 (2021); listed separately).
